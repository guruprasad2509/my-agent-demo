# BMS Thermal Requirements (dummy)

| ID | Requirement | ASIL |
|----|-------------|------|
| REQ-BMS-001 | Charging power shall be derated linearly from 45 C to 55 C cell temperature. | B |
| REQ-BMS-002 | Charging shall stop (0 kW) at or above 55 C. | C |
| REQ-BMS-003 | Invalid temperature readings (below -40 C or above 125 C) shall be treated as a sensor fault and charging stopped. | C |
| REQ-BMS-004 | Thermal state shall be published on the vehicle bus every 100 ms. | QM |
